# Twangle PRD

**Product Name**: Twangle (Two Tangled Together)  
**Version**: MVP – Couples’ Relationship App  
**Prepared for**: WholeWellness Coaching / Twangle Initiative  
**Date**: September 30, 2025  

---

## 1. Problem Statement
Relationships often stumble not from lack of love but from misunderstandings, attachment wounds, and misaligned expectations. Couples lack accessible, affordable tools that combine research-backed insights with practical, daily relationship-building practices.  

Twangle seeks to bridge this gap by providing an engaging, interactive platform where couples can:  
- Understand their attachment styles.  
- Identify red flags and growth opportunities.  
- Receive AI-powered relationship coaching.  
- Build fun, restorative experiences (like DIY retreats).  

---

## 2. Goals & Objectives
- **Primary Goal**: Help couples move toward secure attachment and stronger partnerships.  
- **Objectives**:  
  - Deliver actionable insights from psychological frameworks (attachment theory, relationship science).  
  - Foster engagement through gamified assessments, coaching chatbots, and retreat planning.  
  - Create a safe, private digital environment for couples’ exploration.  
  - Support scalability into a subscription-based wellness ecosystem.  

---

## 3. Target Users
- **Primary Users**:  
  - Couples seeking growth and conflict resolution.  
  - Individuals curious about their relational patterns.  
- **Secondary Users**:  
  - Life coaches and counselors exploring supplemental tools.  
  - Faith-based communities emphasizing secure, Christ-like relationship principles.  

---

## 4. Core Features
1. **Attachment Style Assessment**  
   - 25–40 question quizzes.  
   - Immediate results with visualization (secure, anxious, avoidant, fearful).  
   - Red-flag detection for narcissistic tendencies or toxic patterns.  

2. **AI Relationship Coach**  
   - Ongoing chat sessions.  
   - Personalized advice based on assessment results.  
   - End-of-session summaries saved and optionally emailed.  

3. **DIY Couples Retreat Builder**  
   - Generate weekend retreat outlines.  
   - Include prayer, activities, exercises, and journaling.  
   - Gamified completion rewards (badges, progress milestones).  

4. **Community & Sharing** (Phase 2)  
   - Optional peer stories or “Journey to Secure Attachment” groups.  
   - Guided by moderators to maintain confidentiality and Christ-centered values.  

5. **Admin Tools**  
   - Coach dashboards for certified WholeWellness coaches.  
   - Session summaries and aggregated (non-identifiable) insights.  

---

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements
- **Privacy & Security**: No identifiable data stored without consent. Encrypted DB (Supabase + Postgres).  
- **Performance**: Real-time chat response within 2–3 seconds.  
- **Scalability**: Support 10k concurrent users with minimal latency.  
- **Cross-platform**: Mobile-first (Replit prototype → Vercel/Wix deployment).  

---

## 6. Success Metrics
- 60% of users complete full assessments.  
- 40% return for at least three sessions.  
- 25% conversion to subscription tier after 30 days.  
- Positive Net Promoter Score (>20).  

---

## 7. Roadmap
- **Phase 1 (MVP, Q4 2025)**  
  - Assessment quizzes (25Q, later 40Q).  
  - AI chat coach.  
  - Simple retreat builder.  

- **Phase 2 (Q1 2026)**  
  - Secure community sharing.  
  - Gamified growth pathways.  
  - Expanded database of retreat activities.  

- **Phase 3 (Q2–Q3 2026)**  
  - Coach dashboard integration.  
  - Subscription management & marketplace for certified coaches.  
  - API for churches/organizations to integrate with Twangle.  

---

## 8. Risks & Challenges
- **Psychological boundaries**: App must clarify it is not therapy.  
- **User trust**: High need for confidentiality and transparency.  
- **Engagement**: Couples may resist assessments if too long or “clinical.”  
- **Monetization**: Balancing free vs. paid features without alienating users.  

---

## 9. Costs & Resources
- **Development**: ~400 build hours (118 UI components, 20+ DB tables).  
- **Infrastructure**: Supabase + Vercel ($25–100/month at MVP scale).  
- **Marketing**: Social campaigns, couples’ workshops, church partnerships.

# Wireframe Flow Addendum

## 1. High-Level Flow
**Entry → Assessment → Results → AI Coach → Retreat Builder → Save/Share**

---

## 2. Wireframe Screens (MVP)

### Screen 1: Welcome & Onboarding  
- Branding: “Twangle – Two Tangled Together”  
- Short tagline: *Discover your patterns. Build your bond.*  
- CTA: [Start Assessment]  
- Optional: “Already have results? Jump to Coach”  

---

### Screen 2: Attachment Assessment  
- Multiple-choice questions (25–40 items).  
- Progress bar (“Q12 of 25”).  
- Tone: conversational, not clinical.  
- CTA after final Q: [See Results]  

---

### Screen 3: Results Dashboard  
- Graphical “Attachment Style Meter” (pie chart or bar segments: secure, anxious, avoidant, fearful).  
- Short text explanation.  
- Optional red flag banner: “Indicators suggest potential narcissistic traits — review with caution.”  
- CTA: [Talk to AI Coach] or [Plan a Retreat]  

---

### Screen 4: AI Relationship Coach  
- Chat interface.  
- Suggested prompts: “Help us with communication,” “Plan our next date night.”  
- Output: empathetic, structured responses.  
- End-of-session button: [Save Summary] (stored to DB + optional email).  

---

### Screen 5: DIY Retreat Builder  
- Input: preferred location (home, park, weekend away).  
- Options:  
  - Spiritual focus (prayer, scripture journaling).  
  - Emotional focus (trust-building exercises).  
  - Fun focus (games, adventures).  
- Output: 1–2 day itinerary, broken into blocks (morning/afternoon/evening).  
- CTA: [Save Retreat]  

---

### Screen 6: Save & Share  
- User can save results, retreat plans, and chat summaries.  
- Option to export to PDF or email.  
- For couples: invite partner link.  

---

## 3. Technical Notes for Dev Handoff
- **Frontend**: React (Replit prototype), mobile-first.  
- **Backend**: Supabase (auth + DB).  
- **Data tables**:  
  - `users` (profile, auth).  
  - `assessments` (questions, answers, results).  
  - `sessions` (AI coach chats, summaries).  
  - `retreats` (plans, exports).  
- **Integrations**: OpenAI API for chat; Supabase functions for summary storage.  

---

## 4. User Journey Example
1. Monica and Charles download app.  
2. Each takes assessment → results show anxious + dismissive mix.  
3. Twangle AI Coach suggests “daily check-in ritual.”  
4. They build a weekend retreat with activities like prayer walks + gratitude journaling.  
5. Retreat saved and emailed as a PDF.
