# Couples Relationship Strengthening Questions

## Emotional Intimacy
> Go gently—name one feeling and where it shows up.

1. What is one emotion you felt strongly this week, and when did you notice it first?
2. How do you physically experience sadness or happiness in your body?
3. Is there a moment recently when you felt understood by your partner? Describe how that felt.
4. Which emotion is easiest for you to share, and which is hardest? Why?
5. When do you most need emotional support from your partner?
6. How do you show love when words are difficult to find?
7. What helps you feel safe to share vulnerable feelings?
8. How do you react when your partner expresses a strong feeling?
9. What is one thing that helps you calm down during emotional stress?
10. When did you last feel joy together, and what sparked it?
11. What feeling do you struggle to name or express? Where do you feel it in your body?
12. How can your partner support you when you are anxious?
13. What is an emotional ‘green flag’ in your relationship?
14. How do you know when your partner is hurt (without them saying it)?
15. Is there a way you’d like to express affection more openly?
16. Describe a time you felt appreciated by your partner.
17. What would help you feel more comfortable sharing your feelings?
18. How does your partner make you feel loved emotionally?
19. What emotional need do you think is unmet in your relationship?
20. How do you celebrate each other’s emotional growth?

## Communication Conflict
> Focus on one moment; describe impact, not blame.

1. Describe a recent disagreement—what did you feel and need in that moment?
2. What words help you feel heard during a conflict?
3. How can you express hurt without placing blame?
4. What is one thing your partner does that helps you stay calm in arguments?
5. How would you like your partner to respond when you’re upset?
6. Is there a phrase that diffuses tension for you?
7. Share a positive outcome that arose from a past conflict.
8. What communication habit would you like to change?
9. How do you feel when feedback is given to you?
10. What’s one way you resolve misunderstandings quickly?
11. How do you start difficult conversations?
12. Is there a communication pattern from your childhood influencing you now?
13. How do you express ‘I’m sorry’ genuinely?
14. What does respectful disagreement look like for you?
15. How do you communicate your needs under stress?
16. What is one way your partner could improve listening skills?
17. When do you feel defensive, and how can you manage it?
18. What tone or body language helps you feel safe?
19. How do you repair after a fight?
20. What makes you trust your partner during tense discussions?

## Physical Intimacy
> Use clear, kind language for touch you welcome.

1. What type of touch feels most comforting?
2. How do you prefer your partner to initiate physical closeness?
3. Is there a new way you’d like to experience physical intimacy?
4. How can you express boundaries around physical touch?
5. What physical gesture always makes you smile?
6. How do you communicate a desire for more closeness?
7. What makes you feel safe during intimacy?
8. How has your comfort with physical affection changed?
9. Is there a touch you’d like to try—or avoid?
10. What role does non-sexual touch play for you?
11. How do you express appreciation physically?
12. When do you feel most relaxed together?
13. How often would you like to be physically close?
14. How does affection help you feel connected?
15. What kind physical gesture means ‘I love you’ to you?
16. How do you handle differences in physical needs?
17. What is an intimate moment you’d like to repeat?
18. How can your partner make you feel desired?
19. What nonverbal signals do you use for physical comfort?
20. How would you like to show more physical affection?

## Finances Planning
> Think in pictures—what does ‘secure’ look like day-to-day?

1. What does financial security look like in your relationship?
2. How do you picture your future together regarding money?
3. What daily habits help you feel secure about finances?
4. How do you talk about unexpected expenses?
5. How can you support each other’s savings goals?
6. What is one thing you’d like to change about budgeting?
7. How do you celebrate financial milestones?
8. What worries you about money, and how do you cope?
9. How do you plan big purchases or trips together?
10. What does ‘enough’ mean to you financially?
11. How do you handle disagreements about spending?
12. What is a money management skill you want to improve?
13. How do you make financial decisions as a team?
14. What is one thing you wish your partner understood about your financial mindset?
15. How do financial values affect your trust in each other?
16. What does generosity look like in your daily life?
17. How do you balance fun and financial responsibility?
18. What’s a financial tradition you want to start together?
19. How has your view of money changed in this relationship?
20. What small step can you take to feel more financially secure?

## Values Spiritual
> Share what gives meaning; one practice you want together.

1. What brings you a sense of purpose in your life?
2. How do you experience spiritual connection together?
3. Is there a daily ritual that grounds you or inspires you?
4. Share a tradition that has deep meaning for you.
5. What belief gives you hope when times are hard?
6. How can you support each other’s spiritual journeys?
7. Describe a moment you felt spiritually connected with your partner.
8. What does forgiveness mean to you?
9. How do you express gratitude daily?
10. Is there a value or principle you want to grow together?
11. What spiritual or cultural practice would you like to try as a couple?
12. How do you celebrate meaning or purpose together?
13. Is there an inspiring story or text you want to share or reflect on?
14. How do you work through spiritual differences in your relationship?
15. What helps you feel at peace with your partner?
16. What is one value you would like your children or future generations to inherit?
17. How do you talk about existential or philosophical questions together?
18. What gives you a sense of belonging?
19. How can your partner help remind you of what matters most?
20. What shared dream or vision inspires you both?

## Play Adventure
> Choose something light you can actually do this week.

1. What is one light-hearted activity you want to do together this week?
2. Describe a silly moment you would love to recreate.
3. What is your favorite way to play as a couple?
4. When was the last time you tried something new together?
5. Is there an adventure you’ve dreamed of taking together?
6. How can you make everyday routines more fun?
7. Share a game you’d like to play with your partner.
8. What does adventure look like in your relationship?
9. How do spontaneous plans make you feel?
10. What’s a creative project you could try together?
11. Describe a place you’d like to explore together.
12. When did you last laugh until you cried together?
13. Do you have a favorite tradition for fun or play?
14. How can you encourage each other to loosen up and play?
15. What is a playful challenge you want to take on?
16. How does adventure help you feel closer?
17. What activity helps you both forget stress and live in the moment?
18. How can you inject more surprise into your week?
19. What is a childhood game you’d like to play again?
20. How do you show your playful side to your partner?

## Trust Boundaries
> Name a boundary that protects connection, not distance.

1. What boundary helps you feel safe and connected?
2. How do you communicate new boundaries to your partner?
3. Describe a moment when your partner honored your boundary.
4. How do you know a boundary is working for your relationship?
5. How do you set a boundary with kindness?
6. Share a trust-building habit in your relationship.
7. What is a boundary you’ve struggled to keep?
8. How do you repair trust after a breach?
9. What does mutual respect look like in day-to-day life?
10. How do you want to be supported in honoring your boundaries?
11. How can boundaries help grow intimacy, not distance?
12. What is one way you show trust in your partner?
13. How do you handle differences in comfort zones?
14. What is a past experience that shaped your views on trust?
15. How do you recover when trust is challenged?
16. How do you negotiate boundaries when they conflict?
17. Describe one thing your partner does that strengthens your trust.
18. How do you talk about secrets or privacy in your relationship?
19. What does forgiveness mean to you in the context of trust?
20. How would you like to celebrate moments of mutual trust?
