Of course! Creating connection-building questions based on the work of Drs. John and Julie Gottman and Secure Attachment Theory is a fantastic foundation for a couples app. The goal is to move couples from judgment to curiosity and from conflict to connection.

Here is a wide range of conversation prompts focused on common problem areas, designed to foster understanding and intimacy.

## 🗺️ Building Love Maps: Getting to Know Each Other Again
A core Gottman concept, "Love Maps," is about having a detailed understanding of your partner's inner world. These questions refresh that knowledge.

What is one thing that stressed you out this week that I might not know about?

What’s a small, simple thing that would make you feel incredibly loved by me right now?

What’s a dream you've had lately, either during sleep or for your future?

Who are the most important people in your life right now, and what challenges are they facing?

What is one of your favorite memories from our time together? What did you feel in that moment?

Is there a new hobby or interest you’d like to explore if you had more time?

## ❤️ Fostering Fondness & Admiration
This is the antidote to contempt, one of Gottman's "Four Horsemen." It involves actively looking for the good and expressing appreciation.

Tell me about a time you felt really proud of me.

What is one quality you have that you're most proud of?

When we first met, what was one thing that made you think, "I like this person"?

Describe one thing I did recently that made you smile.

If you had to describe me to a stranger in three words, what would they be?

What's one small thing I do that you'd really miss if I stopped doing it?

## 🤝 Strengthening Secure Attachment: Being a Safe Haven
Secure attachment is built on trust and knowing your partner is there for you. These questions reinforce that feeling of safety and reliability.

When you're feeling anxious or overwhelmed, what is the most helpful thing I can do?

Is there anything you feel hesitant to tell me? What would make you feel safer sharing it?

How can I be a better "secure base" for you—someone who encourages you to chase your goals and dreams?

Describe a time when you felt I truly had your back.

Do you feel you can come to me with any problem, no matter how messy? What helps you feel that way?

What does "support" look like and feel like to you on a difficult day?

## 🔥 Navigating Conflict & Common Challenges
These prompts are designed to be "soft startups" to difficult conversations, focusing on common friction points like finances, chores, and intimacy.

On Money & Finances
What did you learn about money growing up, and how do you think it affects us today?

What does financial security mean to you? Let's describe our ideal picture.

What is one financial goal we could work toward together that would feel exciting?

When we talk about money, what is a fear that sometimes comes up for you?

On Sex & Intimacy
What makes you feel most desired and wanted by me (both in and out of the bedroom)?

Is there a type of non-sexual physical touch you wish we shared more often? (e.g., hugs, holding hands, back rubs)

What's one of your favorite sexual memories of us? What made it special?

Is there something new or different you’ve been curious to explore together intimately?

On Chores & Responsibilities
Which household task drains you the most and feels the least appreciated?

How can we make managing our home feel more like a team effort and less like a list of chores?

Is there a weekly "reset" ritual we could create to tackle household tasks together in a fun way?

When you look around our home, what makes you feel peaceful and happy? How can we create more of that feeling?

## 🌉 Creating Shared Meaning & Dreams
This is the highest level of Gottman's "Sound Relationship House." It's about building a life together that feels meaningful.

What is one adventure, big or small, that you want to have with me in the next year?

Are there any family traditions from your childhood you’d like to bring into our life?

What does a perfect, relaxing weekend look like to you? How can we make that happen soon?

Looking ahead 5 years, what do you hope is different about our life together? What do you hope is exactly the same?

What kind of impact do we want to have on the people around us?

What is a dream for your life that you haven't told me about yet?

Tips for Using These Prompts in Your App
Set the Mood: Encourage users to put their phones down (after reading the prompt!), make eye contact, and dedicate a few minutes without distractions.

No Fixing, Just Listening: Remind users the goal is to understand, not to problem-solve or rebut. The speaker's feelings are valid, even if you disagree with their perspective.

Show Empathy: Encourage phrases like, "That makes sense," "Tell me more about that," or "I can see why you'd feel that way."

Take Turns: Both partners should have the chance to answer the same question.

Stay Curious: Approach your partner's answer with genuine curiosity, as if you're getting to know them for the first time.
